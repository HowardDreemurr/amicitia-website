Amicitia Letterforms is a modified subset of Fraunces (J, f, j only).
It retains original upper serifs and italic angle; upright f has a slightly wider advance for its repositioned head.
J/j have simple bottom bowls without an S bend or ball terminal. Upright f retains the original ball terminal, moved right to meet a straight stem. Italic f uses the upright f head, proportioned to its stem and slanted at the original italic angle. Its complete original italic tail is retained, including the original rounded terminal; only its head is replaced. See scripts/refine_letters.py.
Uppercase F and all other letters are rendered by the original Fraunces font.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces
Source files: Fraunces[SOFT,WONK,opsz,wght].ttf and Fraunces-Italic[SOFT,WONK,opsz,wght].ttf
Source settings: opsz=14, SOFT=0, WONK=1; weights 400, 500, 600, 700.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see Fraunces-OFL.txt.
The derivative uses a distinct family name: Amicitia Letterforms.

To regenerate: install fonttools[woff] and brotli, download the upstream TTFs
as Fraunces.ttf and Fraunces-Italic.ttf alongside Fraunces-OFL.txt into a
source directory, then run:
  python scripts/refine_letters.py SOURCE_DIRECTORY public/fonts
Copy SOURCE_DIRECTORY/letter-alternates.css into the app directory.
SOURCE_DIRECTORY/refined-letters.svg provides a before/after outline proof.
